Frequently Asked Questions

Can I upgrade my tent pitch to a glamping dome before arrival?
Yes, you can upgrade your campsite zone or add guided trail activities instantly via your guest dashboard up to 24 hours before check-in.
How does the Weather-Flex Guarantee work?
If severe mountain weather or high wind advisories close the trails during your booked dates, you can reschedule your stay for free or receive a 100% refund on your pass.
What camping gear is included with my reservation?
Glamping and Safari Tent passes include pre-pitched weatherproof tents, insulated bedding, lanterns, and firewood. Pinewood Pitch guests can also rent sleeping bags and stoves via the guest portal.
Are the camp rangers certified for backcountry safety?
Absolutely. Every trail leader and river guide holds Wilderness First Responder (WFR) certification and carries direct basecamp satellite communication.
